# Lessons for every genre

A lesson moves here when it holds beyond one genre. Each one names the genre lessons it came from.

**principles/1 — A purchase needs a loss to restore.** Lives, time, moves or progress that a player can lose
are what purchases and rewarded ads sell back. A game with no way to fail earns from ads alone. From
`arrow-escape/1`, `arrow-escape/11`.

**principles/2 — New mechanics carry retention.** Players stay while they learn. Plan when each new mechanic
arrives, not only how hard each level is. From `arrow-escape/7`, `arrow-escape/8`.

**principles/3 — Protect the first sessions.** No interstitial ads and no feature clutter in the first levels;
unlock features one by one. From `arrow-escape/9`.

**principles/4 — Lower what the player must scan.** Reveal big boards in parts and make untappable pieces look
untappable. From `arrow-escape/4`, `arrow-escape/5`.

**principles/5 — Copy the proven core, win on execution.** A core action that already sells is a smaller risk
than a new one. The gain is in the curve, the fail state, the economy and the ads. From `grand-games/1`,
`grand-games/6`.
